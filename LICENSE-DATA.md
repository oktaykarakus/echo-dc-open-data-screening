The **data files** (`data/`, `*/results/`, `*/data/`, `07_manuscript_assets/Tables/`) are derived from OpenStreetMap (inventory records, anchor loads, strategic roads) and are therefore published under the **Open Database Licence (ODbL) 1.0**; the licence text is in `LICENSE-ODbL-1.0.txt`. Under the ODbL, anyone who publicly uses these files or a database derived from them must credit OpenStreetMap, make clear that the data are available under the ODbL, and keep derived databases under the ODbL.
